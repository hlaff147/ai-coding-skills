# Daily Standup Summary (GitHub Copilot)

When asked to generate daily standup updates, status reports, or Slack/Teams standup summaries:

1. **Format Output**:
   - Organize into three clear sections:
     - **Ontem (Yesterday)**: Key completed items, MRs merged or submitted.
     - **Hoje (Today)**: Primary focus for the day and target milestones.
     - **Bloqueios (Blockers)**: Actionable impediments or dependencies on other teams/reviews.

2. **Style**:
   - Keep entries concise and outcome-driven.
   - Use action verbs.
   - Avoid trivial activities (e.g. "checked emails").
